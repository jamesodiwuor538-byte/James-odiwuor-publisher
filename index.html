How to order

    Tap "Order this book" under the title you want.

About James

James Odiwuor is a Kenyan writer and publisher. He writes to share knowledge, encouragement and meaningful stories with readers, and publishes his own books so they can reach people directly.

His aim is simple: to get more people reading, and to help them grow through what they read.
